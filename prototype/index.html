    What the technobabble really is
    
    
    
      
    Emitter
    One qubit per ship. Until it fires it is in a superposition of a shield state (0) and a lance state (1).
    
      
    Bell-lock
    Entanglement. Locking on ties the two emitters into one shared quantum state, so your tuning changes what their emitter does.
    
      
    Lance power
    A rotation of the qubit between 0 and 1. It sets the odds of collapsing lance-side.
    
      
    Shield phase and lance phase
    The qubit's phase. Phase changes no odds on its own. Through the Bell-lock it makes the two tunings interfere, which is how a shield can turn a lance around.
    
      
    Collapse
    Measurement. Each round is measured once, and the result is random with the odds shown in the grid.
    
      
    Beam energy and barrier height
    Quantum tunnelling. A particle gets through a barrier more easily the higher its energy and the lower the barrier. Each ship has a rating for both, and yours can be refitted between battles.
    
      
    Probe scan
    Measurement back-action. Reading the emitter's power disturbs its phase, the property the probe did not measure. No probe can read both cleanly.
    
      
    Coherence storm
    Decoherence. Outside noise disturbs the emitters and breaks the entanglement. Without it, phase has nothing to interfere with and the fight turns classical: raw lance power wins.
